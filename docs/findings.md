## The implicit optimisation target

Across the programmes and exercise systems, the common objective appears to be:

> Maximise useful, sustainable results per unit of time, effort, complexity, and recovery cost.

That favours strength and power which transfer to real activity, repeatable training, adequate rather than maximal volume, simple programme structure, broad physical competence, and adjustment to the trainee's immediate goal.
